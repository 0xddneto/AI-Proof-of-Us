### LangGraph external-effect boundary

Thread: <https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph/issues/7844>

Two reviewers highlighted that host-derived evidence can establish only
host-observed facts. A provider receipt must remain an attributed observation;
the host must not map an opaque receipt ID directly to `delivered`, and a later
bounce must downgrade the derived state. AIPOU incorporated this boundary in
`docs/evidence-boundaries.md` and `docs/framework-lifecycle-adapter.md`.
